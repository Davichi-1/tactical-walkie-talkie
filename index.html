Walkie-Talkie Setup Guide

1. Connect devices

  • Open this site (over https://) on every device.
  • Use the same Room ID everywhere, or tap INVITE and send the link.
  • Pick a callsign, tap CONNECT UNIT and allow the microphone.
  • Up to 10 units per room. Units appear in the list within a few seconds.

2. Talk

  • Units must be on the same channel (01-10) to hear each other.
  • Hold PUSH TO TALK (or hold SPACEBAR on a PC). Release to listen.

3. Tips

  • Use headphones, or keep devices apart, to avoid feedback.
  • Keep the app open and the screen on. Phones pause the mic when the browser is in the background.
