Walkie-Talkie Setup Guide

1. Connecting Squad Units:

  • Share the same Room ID with all squad units.
  • Ensure each participant chooses a custom Callsign and clicks CONNECT UNIT.
  • Units must tune to the same Channel (01 - 10) to transmit and receive.

2. How to Transmit:

  • Press and HOLD the green PUSH TO TALK button (or hold SPACEBAR on PC) to speak.
  • Release when finished to clear the line.
